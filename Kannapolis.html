About Kannapolis

Kannapolis is located primarily in Cabarrus County, with a portion of the city reaching into Rowan County. It has reinvented itself in recent years with a refreshed downtown and the North Carolina Research Campus. Kannapolis also remains closely tied to its racing and textile heritage.

Why people choose Kannapolis

  • A revitalized downtown with dining and community events
  • The North Carolina Research Campus and related employers
  • Home options across many styles and price points
  • Straightforward access to Charlotte and Interstate 85
