About Harrisburg

Harrisburg is a growing community in Cabarrus County, east of Concord and within reach of the Charlotte area. It is known for newer neighborhoods, parks and a quieter pace than the city.

Why people choose Harrisburg

  • Newer subdivisions and a range of home sizes
  • Parks and open space close to home
  • Reasonable access to Concord, Charlotte and nearby employers
  • A growing set of local shops and restaurants
