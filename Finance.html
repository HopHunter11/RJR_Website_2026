About financing

We treat each customer as an individual, not a number.

We help place you into a loan profile based on standard banking guidelines, and we use common sense to help you obtain the best loan possible. We work with a wide range of lenders offering competitive rates and a variety of mortgage products and strategies tailored to your needs.

Why get pre-qualified?

  1. Pre-qualification is a dry run of the loan application. A lender uses details you provide about credit, income, assets and debts to estimate how much mortgage you can afford. It can take minutes to a few hours, and it is free.
  2. A pre-qualification is non-binding because the lender has not verified your information. It still signals your general creditworthiness to sellers.
  3. Most sellers will not accept an offer without at least a pre-approval letter, so this is the first step if you are serious about buying.
