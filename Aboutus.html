About us

Serving Charlotte metro home buyers and sellers since 2002.

RJR Real Estate Group was founded in 2002. As the firm has grown, it has partnered with individuals and businesses to provide diverse real estate, consulting, and investment expertise for our clients in the Carolinas.

We help property owners market their homes, and we serve home buyers, owners and investors.

No matter how large or small your real estate needs, our experienced team will work to deliver strong results.
